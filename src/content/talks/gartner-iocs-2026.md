Sovereign AI isn't about distrusting cloud or AI vendors. It's about knowing where your keys are, and who else can turn them. Ownership and control are not the same thing, and many solution providers conflate them. Open source gives you the master key, replacing blind trust with full visibility. Drawing on lessons from the virtualization shakeup, this talk helps you map dependencies, build exit paths, and maintain true control over your AI stack.
